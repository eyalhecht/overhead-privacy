# Overhead privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Overhead shows you where the plane passing over you is flying from and to, on your iPhone's Lock Screen. This page explains what the app collects, why, and how long it is kept.

## What Overhead collects

- **A rough location.** Your phone rounds its location to about 100 metres before it is saved on the phone or sent anywhere. Your exact location never leaves your phone.
- **A device ID.** This is Apple's random identifier for our apps on your phone. It is not your advertising ID, and it is not linked to your name.
- **Push tokens.** Apple gives these to the app so our server can show and update the Lock Screen card.
- **Your settings.** Whether cards are on, whether quiet hours are on, and your time zone.

Overhead has no accounts. It does not collect your name, email, contacts or exact location. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Why

Only to find the planes passing near you and show them on your Lock Screen.

## Who receives it

- **Our server** (Google Cloud, United States) stores the data above.
- **adsb.fi and adsb.lol**, which provide flight positions, receive the rough location, without any identifier, when the server asks which planes are nearby.
- **Apple's push notification service** receives the push tokens and the card's flight details.
- **Flightradar24** receives flight numbers only, to look up routes. No data about you.

We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

## How long it is kept

- **On our server:** if your phone hasn't checked in for 30 days (for example, after you delete the app), all its data is deleted automatically.
- **Server logs:** kept for 14 days. They contain a shortened device ID and flight events, never your location.

## Your choices

- Turn cards off in the app's Settings.
- Turn quiet hours on or off in the app's Settings.
- Turn off location for Overhead in iOS Settings at any time.
- Delete the app, and your data is removed from our server within 30 days.
