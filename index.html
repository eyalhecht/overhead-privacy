Overhead privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Overhead shows you where the plane passing over you is flying from and to, on your iPhone's Lock Screen. This page explains what the app collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and your rights.

Who is responsible

Eyal Hecht, Hakabaim 19, Ramat Gan, Israel. Email: eyalhe3@gmail.com. See also the legal notice.

What Overhead collects

  • A rough location. Your phone rounds its location to about 100 metres before it is saved on the phone or sent anywhere. Your exact location never leaves your phone. The server keeps only the last rough location, no history.
  • A device ID. Apple's random identifier for our apps on your phone. It is not your advertising ID and is not linked to your name. Storing and reading it is strictly necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • Push tokens. Apple gives these to the app so our server can show, update and end the Lock Screen card.
  • Your settings. Whether cards are on, whether quiet hours are on, and your time zone.
  • When your phone last checked in, and the state of your current card.

Overhead has no accounts. It does not collect your name, email, contacts or exact location. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, so "Do Not Track" settings change nothing.

Sharing your location is your choice, but without it Overhead cannot find planes near you.

Why, and on what legal basis

Only to find the planes passing near you and show them to you, which is the service you ask for by installing and setting up the app (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Nothing is used for anything else.

Who receives it

  • Our server, hosted by Google Cloud in Frankfurt, Germany, stores the data above. Google acts as our processor under its data processing terms.
  • Apple's push notification service receives the push tokens and the card's flight details, to deliver the card.
  • Flight data providers receive nothing about you. Our server downloads the planes for large fixed map areas and does the matching to your location itself. Routes come from a copy of a public route list kept on our server.

Like any internet service, our server sees your IP address when the app connects. It is not logged or stored.

Google and Apple may process data outside the EU, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell or share your personal information with anyone.

How long it is kept

  • Delete my data in the app's Settings removes everything on our server about your phone at once.
  • If your phone hasn't checked in for 30 days (for example, after you delete the app), all its data is deleted automatically.
  • Phones that cannot show a card (cards off and the app closed) are not checked against flights; their stored data is still deleted after 30 days.
  • Server logs are kept for 14 days. They contain a shortened device ID and flight events, never your location.
  • On your phone, the last rough location and your settings stay until you delete your data or the app.

Your rights

  • You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or receive a copy of your data, and object to its use (GDPR Art. 15 to 21; Israel's Protection of Privacy Law gives similar rights to access and correct).
  • The quickest way to delete everything is Delete my data in the app's Settings. For anything else, email eyalhe3@gmail.com.
  • You can complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live, or the Israel Privacy Protection Authority.
  • Turn cards off, turn quiet hours on or off, or turn off location for Overhead in iOS Settings at any time.

Children

Overhead is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and important changes are announced in the app.
